Dibus Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Data controller: SoftApp Solutions LLC

In short: Dibus creates no accounts, has no analytics, shows no ads and bundles no third-party SDKs. Your drawings stay on your device or, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud, and we never receive them. What you type for a magic page is processed by Apple Intelligence, on your device or on Apple's servers (Private Cloud Compute), and it does not go through our servers either. From our website, the app only asks for the pack catalog and its pages. This website counts visits with no cookies and without storing your IP address; only Cloudflare, which protects it, may set a technical security cookie. And if you email us, we keep your email so we can reply.

Who is responsible

SoftApp Solutions LLC, the company that develops and publishes Dibus. For anything related to this policy, write to dibus@softapp.tech. If you are in the European Union, the company's full contact details —legal name, postal address, phone number and email— are published on the Dibus App Store product page, as the Digital Services Act requires.

What data we collect

Through the app, none that identifies you. Dibus does not ask you to sign up, does not create accounts, includes no analytics or measurement tools, shows no ads and bundles no third-party kits: only Apple's own system frameworks. We do not use the advertising identifier or any other persistent identifier, and we do not track you in Apple's sense of the word. We do not know your name, your email, or what you draw.

There is exactly one thing the app asks our website for, and we spell it out in the next section because we would rather you read it than discover it. Magic pages, purchases and iCloud sync go through Apple, and we cover them further down too. The only thing that can reach us with your name or address is an email you send us yourself, and we explain that under “If you email us”.

When the app connects to the internet

On launch, Dibus downloads a file from dibus.softapp.tech listing the available packs, and downloads the packs' pages when they appear on screen or when you open them. It is a request like any web page's: it carries no name, no email, no device identifier, and no code that could tell you apart from anyone else. We send no cookies, we do not stamp the app with an installation number, and we build no profiles.

As with any connection, the server unavoidably sees the IP address the device connects from, along with technical details such as the date and the kind of request. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (Microsoft) behind Cloudflare, and both providers keep technical logs for short periods so they can serve the content and protect it from attacks. We do not download those logs, we do not cross-reference them with anything, and we do not use them to identify anyone. The legal basis for that minimal processing is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and the infrastructure secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you would rather the app connected to nothing at all, use it in airplane mode: the pages bundled with the app, your drawings and every coloring tool work offline. Purchases do need a connection, and so do magic pages, except when Apple generates them on the device itself. If you use iCloud, your drawings sync once you are back online.

Magic pages

When you describe a page (“a seahorse among the seaweed”), Dibus asks Apple Intelligence for it through Image Playground, which is part of the operating system. Depending on your device and iOS version, Apple generates the image on the device itself or on Private Cloud Compute, Apple's infrastructure built for this purpose; from iOS 27 on, always on Private Cloud Compute. In that case your description leaves the device for Apple, and Apple states that the data processed there is not stored or made accessible to Apple.

What matters here: that text never reaches SoftApp servers or any third-party artificial intelligence. For magic pages, Dibus uses no OpenAI, no Google, no other AI provider: only the system's own. When Dibus opens the Image Playground window, it only offers Apple's styles; the ChatGPT option does not appear. The resulting page is saved with your drawings, as explained under “Your drawings”. What Apple does with the request is explained on its Apple Intelligence & Privacy page and in Apple's privacy policy.

Ideas go through Apple Intelligence's content filters. If an idea is not appropriate, the page simply is not generated and the app suggests trying something else.

Pack pages are a different matter: we created some of them with the help of Grok, xAI's AI, before putting them in the app, as credited in Dibus's Settings. We did it with our own account and without any of your data: the app never connects to xAI.

Your drawings

If you have iCloud turned on, they are saved in your own iCloud, in a private Dibus folder inside your Apple Account, and they sync across your devices signed in to that same account. Without iCloud, they are saved only on your device, in the app's private folder. Either way, SoftApp cannot see or access them: there is no server of ours in between, and what is in your iCloud is stored by Apple under its privacy policy. If you do not want them to sync, turn off iCloud for Dibus in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud. They stay until you delete them: press and hold a drawing and choose Delete, or open Dibus Settings and tap “Delete all drawings”; with iCloud, they are also deleted on your other devices. If you uninstall the app, they are removed from the device, but the ones in iCloud stay in your account until you delete them from the app or from iCloud storage management in iOS Settings. If you have your device's iCloud backup switched on, that backup may include them; Apple handles that and you control it from iOS Settings.

If you save a drawing to Photos, the app first asks for permission to add images only, not to see your library. The drawing goes into your library and, from that point on, your iCloud settings are in charge, not ours. If you share or print it, it goes wherever you choose.

Purchases

In-app purchases —the page packs, the Kids Collection and the Adult Collection— are handled by Apple through the App Store. SoftApp receives no payment data: no cards, no addresses, no buyer identity. All we get from Apple are aggregated sales reports (how many units, in which country), with no identifiable person behind them. Restoring purchases and Family Sharing are also handled by Apple, under its own privacy policy.

This website

This website uses no third-party analytics tools, and we set no cookies. Cloudflare, which protects the site, may store a technical security cookie in your browser (cf_clearance and, sometimes, __cf_bm) to tell people apart from automated bots. It is not used for tracking or advertising and, since it is needed to keep the site secure, it is exempt from consent. The site has its own visit counter: when you open a page, your browser tells softmetrics.softapp.tech, a server of ours, that a page on dibus.softapp.tech was viewed (at most, with its language) and whether it is the first one of this visit. So the same visit is not counted twice, the browser keeps a mark in its session storage, which clears itself when you close the tab. On the server, all that happens is that the day's totals go up by one: we do not store your IP address or anything that could recognize you. That notice goes through Cloudflare, like the rest of the site.

If you email us

If you write to dibus@softapp.tech, we receive your email address, whatever you tell us in the message and any screenshots you attach. We use it only to reply to you and to sort out what you asked about; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep those emails for as long as the conversation lasts and for up to a year afterwards; then we delete them, or sooner if you ask. They are received and stored on our behalf by our email provider, Proton AG (Switzerland), on its servers in Europe.

Children

Dibus is rated 4+ and is meant for kids and grown-ups alike. It is not in the App Store Kids Category because it includes in-app purchases and links to this website, so an adult should set the device up before handing it to a child.

The Kids | Adults switch in the app only changes which pages are shown. It does not ask for anyone's age, the choice is stored only on the device, and it is not a parental control: purchases and magic pages are available in both modes.

The essential point is simple: the app sends us no personal data from anyone, and therefore none from children. There is no sign-up, no profile, no advertising, no chat, and no way for a child to contact strangers from the app. We designed Dibus not to process data, which is the safest way to respect children's privacy rules such as COPPA in the United States, the GDPR and, in Spain, Organic Law 3/2018.

One thing worth knowing: what a child types for a magic page leaves the device for Apple when the image is generated on Private Cloud Compute, as explained above. It does not reach us, but it is better not to type a name or other personal details there.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to control spending, turn on “Ask to Buy” and Screen Time in iOS Settings. In Screen Time, under content and privacy restrictions, you can also block image creation with Apple Intelligence if you would rather they did not use magic pages. And if you ever suspect we may have received a child's data, write to us: we will check and delete it.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the European Economic Area, the GDPR gives you the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Write to dibus@softapp.tech and we will answer within one month at the latest.

With Dibus the answer will almost always be the same, and it is good news: apart from any emails you have sent us, we hold no data of yours to look up, correct or delete. Our providers' technical logs are not tied to your name and we cannot link them to a specific person, so we could not identify you from them either (Article 11 GDPR). Your drawings and pages are yours to delete from the app itself, whenever you like, without asking us.

There is no consent to withdraw, because the app never asks for any: there is no analytics or advertising to accept. The “save to Photos” permission can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings > Apps > Dibus.

If you think we are getting this wrong, you can lodge a complaint with your country's supervisory authority. In Spain that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

If you are in Brazil (LGPD)

Brazil's General Personal Data Protection Law (Lei nº 13.709/2018, the “LGPD”) also applies to us, and everything this policy says still holds: the app sends us nothing of yours, and the only personal data we can ever hold is the emails you send us. We handle them to reply to you, on the basis of our legitimate interest (Article 7, IX, LGPD); we keep them for as long as the conversation lasts and for up to a year afterwards; we do not sell them or share them with anyone (Proton only stores them on our behalf); and we make no automated decisions about you.

Your rights (Article 18, LGPD). You can ask us, at any time and free of charge, for: confirmation that we process data about you; access to it; correction of incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date data; anonymisation, blocking or deletion of data that is unnecessary, excessive or processed in breach of the LGPD; portability of your data to another provider; and information on who we have shared it with (no one: Proton only stores it for us). You can also object to the processing if we are not complying with the LGPD. The rights tied to consent (Article 18, VI, VIII and IX) do not apply, because we never ask for your consent; even so, if you ask, we delete your emails.

How to use them: write to dibus@softapp.tech. That address is our channel for anything to do with your personal data, both for you and for the ANPD. We confirm whether we hold data about you and give you access to it within 15 days at most (Article 19, LGPD), and answer everything else as soon as we can. If we do not answer you in time, or you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a petition against us with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority, the ANPD (Article 18, §1). You can also go to consumer protection bodies such as Procon (Article 18, §8).

Where data is processed

SoftApp Solutions LLC is incorporated in the United States. The providers hosting this site and the pack catalog, Cloudflare and GitHub (Microsoft), are also US companies and serve the content from servers around the world, the European Union included. Any international transfers that occur are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which both providers are certified. Worth repeating: the only thing that can travel through those providers are technical connection logs — never your drawings or what you type. What you type for a magic page, if it leaves the device, goes to Apple, as explained under “Magic pages”. The emails you send us are stored by Proton AG in Switzerland, a country the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection.

How long data is kept

On our side, only the emails you send us: for as long as the conversation lasts and for up to a year afterwards. The app sends us no personal data, so from the app we keep nothing. Technical connection logs are kept by Cloudflare and GitHub for short periods under their own policies, and we hold no copy of them. The website's visit totals identify no one. Your drawings last on your device, and in your iCloud if you use it, for exactly as long as you decide.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell, share or hand over data to anyone: not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to artificial intelligence services. The only data we can hold are the emails you send us, and only our email provider stores them, on our behalf. What you type for a magic page goes from your device to Apple Intelligence, as described above, without passing through us. There is no “sale” or “sharing” of personal information in the sense of California privacy law or any other.

Changes to this policy

If it ever changed, we would publish the new version here with its date, and if the change were significant we would say so inside the app too. The plan is for it to change very little.
