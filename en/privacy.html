Privacy

Dibu Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026 · Data controller: SoftApp Solutions LLC

In short: Dibu collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and nothing you do in the app leaves your device or your iCloud.

What data we collect

None. Dibu does not ask you to sign up, does not create accounts, includes no analytics or measurement tools, and shows no ads. We do not know your name, your email, your device, or what you draw.

Magic pages

When you describe a page (“an astronaut seahorse”), Dibu generates it on the device itself using Apple Intelligence. The text you type or dictate is not sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.

Color your photo

Photos you turn into coloring pages are processed entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Neither the original photo nor the resulting page leaves the device. Dibu only accesses the photo you pick each time.

Your drawings

Drawings are saved on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, in your private iCloud database, which only you can access with your Apple Account. SoftApp cannot see or access them. You can delete them at any time from the app itself (see Support).

Purchases

In-app purchases (the page packs and “Unlimited Magic”) are handled by Apple through the App Store. SoftApp receives no payment data: no cards, no addresses, no buyer identity. Restoring purchases and Family Sharing are also handled by Apple.

Children

Dibu is suitable for children. No data is collected from children, or from anyone. Sharing and buying are protected by a parental gate.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell or share data with anyone, simply because we have none.

Changes to this policy

If it ever changed, we would publish the new version here with its date. The plan is for it not to change.
