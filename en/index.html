Magic pages

Whatever you imagine, ready to color

Type or dictate what you feel like — “a seahorse among the seaweed”, “a digger with wings” — and Dibus draws the page in seconds.

  • Created by Apple Intelligence, on your device or on Apple's servers.
  • Never goes through our servers or any third-party AI.
  • Goes through Apple's content filters.

Magic pages require an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M1 or A17 Pro chip. On every other device, Dibus works just the same with the page packs.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing in the way.

Tap to fill

Tap an area and it fills itself, never outside the lines, even in detailed mandalas and stained glass.

Page packs

Mandalas, stained glass, optical illusions, Japan, Christmas, botanical pages and cities, plus dinosaurs, pirates, robots and space.

Apple Pencil and iPad

Pen, marker and eraser, with undo always at hand. Draw with Apple Pencil or your finger and zoom in for the details. On iPad, Dibus is a drawing desk.

Print and save

Print your drawing with AirPrint to keep coloring on paper, and save it to Photos with one tap.

No ads, no tracking

Not a banner, not a video to skip, not a single piece of data collected. Just drawing.
