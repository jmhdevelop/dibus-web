Magic pages

Whatever you imagine, ready to color

Type or dictate what you feel like — “an astronaut seahorse”, “a digger with wings” — and Dibu draws the page in seconds.

  • Generated right on your iPad or iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server: not the text, not the drawing.
  • Works offline, in the car or on a plane.

Magic pages require an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M1 or A17 Pro chip. On every other device, Dibu works just the same with the page packs and “Color your photo”.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing in the way.

Tap to fill

Tap an area and it fills itself, never outside the lines. Made for small fingers and fast coloring.

Color your photo

Snap a photo of your teddy, your house or your cat and Dibu turns it into a line-art page to color.

Page packs

Sea, dinosaurs, farm, space, vehicles and mandalas for adults. Hand-drawn, with care.

Apple Pencil and iPad

Pressure and tilt brushes, unlimited zoom and a palette by your side. On iPad, Dibu is a drawing desk.

Print and save

Print the blank page or the finished drawing with AirPrint, and save it to Photos with one tap.

No ads, no tracking

Not a banner, not a video to skip, not a single piece of data collected. Just drawing.
