Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

In Dibus, open Settings (the corner icon) and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If nothing shows up, check in iOS Settings that you are signed in to the App Store and try again.

Can I share the packs with my family?

Yes. Every Dibus pack, including Unlimited Magic, supports Apple Family Sharing: up to six people in your family group can use the purchase without paying again.

  1. Check that “Share Purchases” is on for your family group in iOS Settings > your name > Family.
  2. On each device, open Dibus > Settings > Restore Purchases.
Why don't magic pages show up on my iPad?

Magic pages are generated with Apple Intelligence, which is only available on:

  • iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max and every iPhone 16 or later.
  • iPad with an M1 chip or later (iPad Pro, iPad Air) and iPad mini with the A17 Pro chip.

You also need iOS or iPadOS 18.1 or later, Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri, and a supported language. If your device does not meet the requirements, the rest of Dibus works exactly the same: page packs, color your photo, Apple Pencil, print and save.

How do I delete my drawings?

Long-press a drawing in your gallery and choose Delete. To remove them all, open Dibus Settings and tap Delete all drawings. In this version drawings are stored only on your device; uninstalling the app removes them.

How do I print a page?

Open the blank page or the finished drawing, tap the share button and choose Print. It works with any AirPrint-compatible printer. From the same menu you can Save to Photos or send the image via Messages or email.
